## Description

rustyView is the iPhone and iPad companion app for rustyDLNA. Connect to your rustyDLNA server to browse your video library, watch movies, and keep compatible copies ready for offline viewing.

YOUR LIBRARY, WITH YOU
Browse folders, search for a movie, and sort your collection. Open movie details to see artwork, descriptions, chapters, and available audio and subtitles.

WATCH YOUR WAY
Stream from your server with playback controls, ten-second skips, a seekable timeline, playback speed, and fit or fill. Choose from the audio tracks, subtitles, and quality options available for each movie. rustyDLNA prepares compatible streams when needed.

READY FOR OFFLINE
Download a compatible copy and play it later without a network connection. Follow download progress, manage saved copies, and choose whether downloads can use cellular data or wait for Wi-Fi.

PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF
Keep favorites, revisit viewing history, and continue unfinished movies. Your saved library and playback positions stay on your device.

REQUIRES A RUSTYDLNA SERVER
rustyView requires access to a compatible rustyDLNA server and the HTTPS address and sign-in details supplied by its owner. Movies and a hosted server are not included. Available media features depend on your library, server, and device. Separate text subtitles are displayed inside rustyView and do not appear in Picture in Picture or external video output. Online streams support Screen Mirroring; direct AirPlay video handoff is unavailable.
